415: Each source is sequentially excited by a rectangular pulse of width $T_{\delta}$.
416: To acquire measurements after the field settles, $T_{\delta} \geq T_{\tau}+T_s$, where $T_s$ is the gradiometer sampling period and $T_{\tau}$ is the current settling time.
420: Setting $T_{\tau}=5\tau$ allows the transient to decay but lowers the localization frequency.
421: In constant current (CC) mode, closed-loop regulation shortens the transient, allowing $T_{\tau}$ to be set well below $5\tau$, so $T_s$ primarily determines the localization frequency.
